From Ciarán · The first step on the journey

The First Step; on the Journey of a Thousand...

Who am I? Well, you can call me Ciarán Raibeart Óg. Most folks think my middle name is pronounced like Robert. I disagree, so we split the difference and they call me Rab. It doesn't really matter, it's not even my real name. My real name is Kieran Robert Ogg. Doesn't take much more than a couple o' brain cells to see what I did there. Kinda makes you think about the word "username" doesn't it? You're using the computer, and the program that asks for your name. But, you're also using your name. Unless you are one of the very few with a Globally Unique name, then technically you're using someone elses name. In fact, my surname tells you that. Both of them. Ogg/Óg has roots in Scottish Gaelic, roughly equivellant to "young" though it was more likely used like "junior" As in, a child bearing their parent's first name. Did you know that in some cultures they used to give their children awful names, to stop evil spirits (child mortality) from taking them. There was an Emperor who named his child "abandoned shit" but it didn't help. The little shit didn't last three years. How much shit have you put up with that lasted longer than it should have?
